In Between
##########

Time of creation of outlines:
    Spring 2026
Place of the first retreat:
    Brenna, Katowice archdiocese
Place of creation of outlines:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Retreat open for adults and children
Description of the retreat:
    This retreat leads participants through the experience of being "in between": between what is familiar and what is still undiscovered; between personal faith and communal faith; between receiving and giving. Starting from the image of God the Creator, who makes space for the human person, we move through a deconstruction and reconstruction of the image of God toward the calling to co-create spaces of faith for others. This path, inscribed in the symbolism of the letters Alef and Bet, leads to the word "Awinu" -- Our Father -- where intimate relationship with God and the community of believers come together as one.
