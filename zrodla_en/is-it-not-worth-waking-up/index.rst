Is It Not Worth Waking Up?
##########################

Time of creation of outlines:
    Spring 2011
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Gliwice diocese
Place of creation of outlines:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Retreat open for people aged 13 to 23
Description of the retreat:
    Retreat with a strong evangelizing character, at the same time preparing for experiencing the Paschal Holidays. A fragment of the outline reflects their character: "Christianity is taking the bull by the horns, a life full of passion, seeking and finding oneself in places surprising to us. A consistent life, but not by the force of a template that we fill. We tirelessly pursue the Holy Spirit... and thanks to this we end up in new places and spaces....". An attempt to show the Church as a place of self-development, not self-adjustment. An attempt to help regain control over one's own life for participants - defining one's near and far goals, finding motivation in oneself, creating space for rebellion regarding our current life: "I don't want this anymore!".
