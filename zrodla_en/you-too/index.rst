You Too?
#######

Time of creation:
    Fall 2011
Location of first retreat:
    Babice, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of creation:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Open retreats for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    We focused on community as a space for meeting. We fought to show that community is not a place where differences are erased but rather enriched by them. People living in good communities are the salt of the earth and infect others, awaken others. By their very existence they ask the question: "why do they live like this?". We also strongly showed that each of us shapes and creates the community and it is as we are, and not as written in some books. Community is a dream. This dream is not without flaws and weaknesses, but it is real and more valuable than the price of certain disappointments that may come our way.
