Human Spirituality
##################

Time of creation of outlines:
    Spring 2006
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Gliwice diocese
Place of creation of outlines:
    Katowice archdiocese
Target group:
    Parish oasis, from pre-oasis to formation before 3rd degree
Description of the retreat:
    Parish retreat organized by local diaconia aiming at integration and increasing awareness of responsibility for one's community. Taking place during Lent which justifies the topic. The goal of the diaconia was to lead participants to the Sacrament of Penance and Reconciliation.
