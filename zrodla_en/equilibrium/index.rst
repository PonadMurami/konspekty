Equilibrium
###########

Creation time:
    Autumn 2018
Place of the first retreat:
    Kokotek, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of creation:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Open retreat for people aged 13 to 32
Retreat description:
    The retreat helps to see how easily we get lost in secondary matters and teaches us to put seeking the Kingdom first: through freedom, discernment, and truth in choices, all the way to metanoia and readiness for the Lord in the rhythm of Advent and the everyday journey.
