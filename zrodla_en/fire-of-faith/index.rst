Fire of Faith
#############

Time of writing:
    Spring 2012
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of writing:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Open retreat for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    Easter Retreat aimed at preparing people for Pentecost. It focused on the Holy Spirit as the One without whom it is impossible to understand the events we experienced during Holy Week. The key moment of the retreat was the Saturday evening prayer where we asked the Holy Spirit for help in renewing our faith. The framework of the retreat was the thought that we are people of the Upper Room - on the one hand, those who are disciples of Jesus, know the teaching, but on the other hand are afraid to confess Him, we try to live without the power of the Holy Spirit.
