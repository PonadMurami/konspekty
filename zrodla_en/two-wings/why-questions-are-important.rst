Goal of the meeting and introduction for the facilitator
========================================================

This group meeting is placed in the middle of the retreat. This is the moment when we should already have a community formed and be ready to sail out into the deep. It will last over 1.5 hours. Use this time to build a space for personal entry into the topic of each of the participants of Your group. Remember what went smoothly and "caught on", and what was heavy and pass it to the moderator and the diaconia of spirituality in any form even before this evening.

The outline has intellectually more difficult moments (texts from the Book of Leviticus or texts from the 2nd century). I would not want this group meeting to be some form of activating workshops. We are fighting for an atmosphere of free conversation and exchange of thoughts. **We absolutely need such a moment of the retreat where the participants can speak, and not just listen to us**. For this reason, there are over 25 sharing questions in the outline. Do not ask them all (100 minutes / 25 questions / 6 people in the group = 40s per answer if nothing else was done), they are to help You shape the meeting in Your own way.

* creating a space in which participants can say what they think themselves

* answering the question what does it mean to seek the truth?

* showing how important it is to ask ourselves questions regarding our faith

* showing that faith is a process! and that it requires conscious effort

What is this faith (not) about?
===============================

We live in Poland. In a Catholic society where the Christian faith surrounds us from the first days of life. For this reason, many of us never had to think about it much more, it is "obvious". A pity, because for this reason very many matters are unclear and incomprehensible. Observing faith from the side we could draw such conclusions:

    Faith is about saying a poem on three breaths every evening starting with "Our Father". As a believer every seven days you dress differently and go to a large hall where you say what others say trying to keep to the common tact. An important feature of faith is also noting one's bad deeds, to present them every month in a wooden booth and listen to what faith thinks about it. Faith is also a bit like science, which loves to arrange knowledge in the form of lists. So you must know several of them: sacraments, works of mercy regarding the body and soul, church commandments, decalogue, mysteries of the rosary. When you master these things once, you have to remind yourself of them regularly until the end of your life. This is faith.

    Rational observer, 13 years old

* What do you think about this description? What in it is close to You? What would you add?

* Do you try to understand faith, and not just participate in it? In what way?

* What comes easily to You in faith and is "obvious"? (that God loves You? that God created the world? that Jesus rose from the dead?)

* What causes You some difficulties in faith?

This observation of a 13-year-old is stopping at external things (We do not want to say by this that they are irrelevant and we can reject them). It describes what is seen at first glance. It is worth saying openly that many of us looked at faith like this. It is hard to call such faith "faith to the max", about which we spoke after reading the Book of Leviticus.

Let's read another text of a certain observer from 1800 years ago:

    For Christians are not distinguished from the rest of mankind either in locality or in speech or in customs. For they dwell not somewhere in cities of their own, neither do they use some different language, nor practice an extraordinary kind of life. (...) They marry like all other men and they beget children; but they do not cast away their offspring. They have a common table, but not a common bed. They are in the flesh, but they do not live after the flesh. They pass their days on earth, but they are citizens of heaven. They obey the prescribed laws, and at the same time surpass the laws by their lives. They love all men, and are persecuted by all. They are unknown and condemned; they are put to death, and restored to life. They are poor, yet make many rich; they are in lack of all things, and yet abound in all; they are dishonored, and yet in their very dishonor are glorified. They are evil spoken of, and yet are justified; they are reviled, and bless; they are insulted, and repay the insult with honor; they do good, yet are punished as evil-doers. When punished, they rejoice as if quickened into life; they are assailed by the Jews as foreigners, and are persecuted by the Greeks; yet those who hate them are unable to assign any reason for their hatred.

    author unknown, "Epistle to Diognetus", 2nd century.

* Would you like to have such strength that someone would write about You like this?

* Do you see something more in faith than just external gestures? What is it?

* Why is it currently easier for us to meet with the version from the first story and not the second one? (be careful not to turn into discussions preceding the Third Vatican Council :P)

* Why did they live like they lived? (non-sharing question serving to go out with a summary)

Faith is always a mystery of the hearts of believers. We know for sure that the first Christians took faith very seriously (they faced death for confessing it). We also know that they asked very many questions and inquired. We are sure that such faith is possible also now. We have no doubts, however, that "it won't do itself".

.. note:: from 325 to 787, 7 ecumenical councils took place. That is on average every 66 years.

The question constantly strikes: **why did they live like this, what was their motivation?** Christians are people who discovered something very important. This discovery has such power that it transforms their life and their whole surroundings. Christians are people who discovered the truth about God and themselves. We are seekers, people asking questions and listening to answers. This search itself is an experience of faith.

These questions exist, and the answer to them is more important than it seems (For older groups: in the 20th century there were two philosophical currents that very erroneously defined answers to fundamental questions about the meaning of life. Why do we fold hands together and not hold knees?
    Such a way of folding hands in prayer began to spread from the 13th century. This custom was practiced in liturgy and private prayers. This gesture derives from the Germanic form of paying homage, according to which a vassal with hands folded like this approached his liege lord and received from him an external sign of granting a fief. In exchange for the fief the vassal pledged to serve the senior faithfully.

How does holy water differ from Ustronianka?
    Chemically nothing. It can be holy water from Ustronianka! Let's look at the prayer spoken during the blessing of water: “Almighty, eternal God, You want that through water, which sustains life and serves for cleansing, also our souls be cleansed and receive a pledge of eternal life, we ask You, bless this water, with which we will be sprinkled on Your day, Lord. Renew in us the source of your grace and defend our souls and bodies from all evil, so that we may approach You with a pure heart and receive Your salvation. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.” Holy water "transfers" this prayer to persons who use it.

Does what jeans I go to church in matter so much to God?
    Ask him. Seriously. For God it is important that we distinguish festive time from ordinary, because He himself did it (Creation of the World) and commanded us (decalogue). In our culture we distinguish this also through clothing. For God the most important is what you have in your heart. Sometimes, however, what is outside can help us in what is inside us. Do you associate situations that when you dress in a suit/beautiful dress you immediately feel more joyful also in spirit? This is a psychological mechanism that we know and can use for a just cause.

Am I to be good because God says so? or maybe God commands good because it is good?
    Good, truth and beauty are universal values not strictly related to religion nor even more so denomination. To be a good and decent man we do not need the Creator's command. On the ground of pure reason and philosophical reflection we come to the fact that "good is good". It was written in our nature by the Creator.

How do we know that Jesus was born on December 25th?
    We don't know. It's a symbolic date. The winter solstice is December 21-22. The intuition of Christians was to place the celebration of the Savior's birth at the moment when we see that days are getting longer and brightness wins with darkness.

Why do we kneel at most prayers? In what is this attitude better than sitting?
    It is not better. It is a matter of convention, culture and our nature. Kneeling is an expression in our culture of immense giving of respect and attitude of love (how do you expect proposals to look? Without kneeling?). The Western Church likes this attitude, because it decided that it is close to it. If You prefer to pray standing there is no problem. This attitude spread in the Church along with growing rigorism - conviction about how great sinners we are, how very unworthy of meeting with God. With the deepening in the Church of the awareness of God's love and mercy, which is greater than our sin, and manifests itself in that God himself entered among sinners unworthy of meeting him, prayer of praise increases, and a dynamic standing attitude fits it more. Sitting attitude favors listening, therefore in such an attitude we pray with the breviary.

Why do an examination of conscience since Jesus knows anyway what we did?
    Because the examination of conscience is not for Jesus only for us. It is we who need to stop and see a piece of our life and somehow summarize it - evaluate, arouse regret, draw conclusions for the future deciding on changes. We have experience, however, that Jesus is happy when we do this and supports us in this, because he wants our good.

How do we know what Jesus said during prayer in Gethsemane since he was there alone?
    "But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you." (Jn 14:26) God wanted us to know part of the content of this prayer, therefore in a supernatural way (cf. "biblical inspiration") he revealed these words to the evangelists.

How did Noah fit so many animals on an ark built of wood?
    Sorry, but he didn't fit. The Book of Genesis is an allegorical Book in its first 11 chapters. It is a story in which there is Truth, but not concerning science and history only faith. The story of Noah speaks about the fact that man is still responsible for all creation (he got this responsibility in Eden) and does not blow it off (literally :D). It also speaks about the fact that God asks man sometimes for things incomprehensible humanly (build a big boat on dry land?)

Why do we have Eucharistic bread, and not something else, e.g. popcorn?
    Bread is daily and simple. Everyone needs it and has access to it. God is just like that: he wants to be in everyday life under every house, and not only in palaces. So he chose. This sign was very expressive for people from this cultural circle (reference to Jewish Passover). In Poland we have this cultural aspect in common. Bread is the same for us.

How does faith know so much what happens to a human being after death?
    The Holy Spirit told us this, and we have been listening to him intensely for 2000 years. This is a sufficiently long time to ask him about various topics. Besides, our founder died and returned to us. The apostles certainly asked him "how was it there".

Why do we make the sign of the cross with holy water entering the church?
    Holy water in the church primarily refers to the water of baptism. The Sign of the cross made with it is a reminder, and at the same time a renewal of the covenant concluded at baptism with Christ. Confession of faith, which is a condition for fruitful experience of the Eucharist, to which we go, or prayer brought to God. Also, to remind ourselves that we enter a holy place. Symbolically wash what is not holy and be ready for meeting with Jesus.

Why are candles put on the altar since their light is not needed?
    Candle light symbolizes vigilance, presence, and above all the Risen Christ, who is the "light of the world". We will also not hide that it is simply a tradition that remained with us from times when we had not yet tamed electricity as humanity. We like these candles, they help us. The candle itself burns to give others light and warmth - we find in this an important truth about the vocation of each of us.

Why do priests wear a clerical collar? Ba! why do they walk in dresses? :|
    What a ring is for spouses (shows connection, deters persons inclined to look for a life partner) a clerical collar is for priests. As for walking in a "dress" (in the area of the Catholic church generally called a cassock or in the case of a monk a habit), we are similar to judicial institutions. Outfit externalizes our internal identity: metalheads walk in leathers (sorry if I am imprecise in assigning subcultures), skinheads in black and in combat boots. Sometimes it is related to the task or function performed (e.g. uniforms of soldiers or policemen). In some formations it undergoes constant evolution, in others it stops at some moment (cf. guards guarding the royal palace in London). I do not know the full history of the cassock. I expect that at some stage of history this outfit referred directly to current fashion. Then its development stopped and today it strongly distinguishes clergymen from other persons (cf. robes and wigs worn in British courts). Just like the clerical collar it is a clear sign that we are dealing with a clergyman - not suitable as a candidate for a husband, serves with help in spiritual matters.

What does "amen" mean? Why didn't they translate it into Polish?
    from Heb. amen – "let it be", "fidelity". It caught on very strongly and so it stayed. It is also cool to have some common word despite the multitude of languages, right? It builds unity between us. And why weren't such words translated into Polish as: physics, philosophy, music, calculator, computer, clown, umbrella, studies, not mentioning "younger" words such as chat or pen-drive? Yes there were, such attempts in the 18th century, but "it didn't work out" because they boiled down more or less to the fact that following their path today we would say "calculating machine", instead of "calculator, or "electronic brain" instead of computer :D

What sense does saying the same prayer 50 times in 30 minutes have?
    Saying has little sense; only such that diction is practiced. Behind words, however, go intentions and meditation. The rosary allows to concentrate on one thought and seek an answer in it - it has a character, one could say, meditative. But the rosary is not just rattling off "Hail Marys" - each decade is a different Mystery describing the life of Christ - because contrary to appearances this prayer is strongly Christocentric which is evidenced by "Jesus" in the middle of every "Hail Mary". However, here we consider this life from the perspective of Mary, who just like us, was an ordinary human being who had to accept everything through faith.

How is it possible that God knows everything, and yet I have free will?
    St. Augustine answered this and it is one of the more important fruits of his life. We live in time, and this means that we have past, present and future. God and the whole heaven is outside of time. He sees past and future simultaneously. He sees therefore Your current choice and the one you will make in an hour. Changing the observation point does not change the fact that from the perspective of someone living in time they make free choices.

Why does the altar boy hit the gong during transubstantiation?
    Imagine long temples in which Mass is celebrated in Latin. The faithful people at that time not understanding too much pray as they know how in their own way. With a long temple and poor lighting not everyone always knew what the moment of liturgical action was. Bells or a gong made the matter easier. We liked it and left it even though currently in sum one could resign from it. Bluntly: sometimes we like this gong ourselves, because when thoughts drift away (yes! Involved Christians are not always at full concentration at Mass) then such a gong brings to order in a moment :).

Why can't I go to heaven with my dog?
    Because a dog does not have a soul and is not a person.

What does God need our ornate temples for, since in heaven He probably has it infinitely nicer?
    He does not need them. Jesus unequivocally presented his point of view on splendor understood humanly in the 6th chapter of the Gospel of St. Matthew: “Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they neither toil nor spin; yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.“ We build temples in this way because there are believers whom it helps. We want to give God so in our understanding, what is best and noblest (Cf. Jn 12:1-8). We have no doubts, however, that God does not really want anything from us, he wants us.

* Why is faith a process? Why can't it be achieved and put on a shelf in the room and had until the end of life?

We are becoming all the time and will be becoming Christians. Faith is a process. It requires conscious effort and taking a direction (decision). We talked about all this since yesterday to now be able to look at it fully consciously. This process begins like every one: from the first step. Faith will open its mysteries before us.

When one gets to know faith, certain false images are shattered. Similarly as in the case of revealing cards today at the meeting. Truth liberates, because it allows to understand and believe, and not just "blindly follow".

Application and prayer
======================

Talk to one person from the retreat about a question related to faith that you ask yourselves. It can be a friend or colleague, it can be a facilitator or priest. It is not about the other person singing the answer to You, only about the sharing itself.

For persons preparing for confirmation I recommend additionally such a question:
