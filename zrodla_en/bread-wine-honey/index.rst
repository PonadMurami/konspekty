Target group:
    Retreat open for adults and children
Description of the retreat:
    The retreat is a journey through three great pilgrimage festivals – Sukkot, Passover and Shavuot – shown in the light of the Paschal Triduum. It is a time of reflection on gratitude in various dimensions of our life: for the past, which abounds in spiritual heritage and God's providence; for the present, which requires personal experience of faith and engagement in the History of Salvation; and for the future, which God constantly renews, inviting us to grow in His guidance.
