Spaces of Freedom
##################

Time of creation of outlines:
    Spring 2010
Place of first retreats:
    Babice, Gliwice diocese
Place of creation of outlines:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Open retreats for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    The retreat theme was chosen as a response to feedback received after the previous trip. Lenten retreats aimed at deepening the understanding of freedom (freedom is not license). We based them strongly in the context of Holy Week - what kind of freedom does Christ want to offer you? Do you want such freedom? The inspiration was a text by Fr. J. Tischner: "There is no freedom among stones, waters, rains and hailstorms, earthquakes and winds. There is no freedom in the beautiful world of butterflies and in the dangerous nests of snakes. And in man? Is it not a paradox that man is visited by the idea of freedom? Where does it come from? Some fight for freedom, others flee from freedom, but freedom is still a problem. Can freedom in this world be of this world? It is against nature. However, if God is internally free, then everything changes. Created in the image and likeness of God, man must carry within himself this wind that blows within the Holy Trinity"
