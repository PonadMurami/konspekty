Light-Life Movement - What Does It Mean?
########################################

Creation time:
    Autumn 2006
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of creation:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Parish Oases from the entire diocese, main emphasis on people new to the Movement (pre-Oasis)
Retreat description:
    The retreat was assumed to be organized for people who are forming themselves in Oasis but do not go on summer retreats. We operated on two planes. First, the retreat itself had a daily schedule identical to the 15-day retreat, which gave participants a taste of this method and convinced them of it. Secondly, the theme of the retreat concerned the identity of Oasis members, showing that specific consequences flow from belonging to the community. Additional retreats showed broadly the vision of the Movement community as a group that tries to fight for a better world - showing specific tools.
