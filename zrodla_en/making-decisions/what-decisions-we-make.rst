Meeting 1. - What decisions do we make?
*****************************************

.. tags:: content|decisions|Decisions, content|responsibility|Responsibility, content|discernment|Discernment, method|group-work|Group work, method|discussion|Discussion, type|workshop|Workshop

Goal
====

Bringing closer the meaning of "decision". Types and degree of complexity of decisions.

Showing the following decisions:

* material
* moral
* spiritual (why did you go to this retreat?)
* tactical (why did you choose this place where you are sitting now?)
* sentimental

All these types of decisions can be further divided into personal and communal.

Introduction
============

In front of the participants there will be two A4 sheets, one with painted mountains, the other with the sea. On the back of these sheets there will be tasks written out that they have to realize. Each participant will be faced with the choice of one of the sheets, and then will perform the task located on its back.

Task 1 from the first sheet (mountains):

* Imagine that you are going to the mountains, you are leading a 15-person trip. What does your preparation for it look like?

**Goal:**
   We want the trip to be successful, everyone returned from it satisfied and happy.

Task 2 from the first sheet (mountains):

* Swear twice, and then crumple the sheet and throw it at the animator (so that it bounces off his head, if you miss repeat the action).

Task 1 from the second sheet (sea):

* Imagine that you are going on a cruise to Denmark, you are leading a 15-person trip. What does your preparation for it look like?

**Goal:**
   We want the cruise to be successful, everyone returned from it satisfied and happy.

Task 2 from the second sheet (sea):

* Swear twice, and then crumple the sheet and throw it at the animator (so that it bounces off his head, if you miss repeat the action).

Discussion
==========

The first choice mountains or sea comes from the tactical-material-sentimental group? If the choice concerned holidays then: how much money we have, what suits us more, time space allows us to plan holidays only in this place

* Material and tactical decisions are trip and cruise

   * Logistics (choice of route, departure and arrival time, mode of transport, planning some additional attractions on the trail, equipping for possible accidents)

   * Equipment (I take only what is evidently needed for me, what will not burden me too much and will help me survive (of course enjoy the trip) from other things I must give up)

* Moral decisions are throwing a paper

   * I count on people objecting and not carrying out this command, because it's stupid to throw at the animator like that. For safety I added swearing.

Decisions that we discussed so far are simple decisions, life however can complicate making a decision more.

Let's imagine a network of public roads on which we move. At each intersection we must decide where to turn (road network – life; intersection – decisions). Let's trace the life of several people and bring out their intersections.

Proposed characters:

* Abraham (setting out from Ur, sacrifice of Isaac)
* Antigone (human law, or divine law. She buried her brother in accordance with divine law, despite Creon's prohibitions (human law))
* Maximilian Maria Kolbe (decision to sacrifice life)
* Fr. Jerzy Popiełuszko (decision to speak the truth despite unfavorable consequences)
* Jesus Christ
* Me (Have I made any decisions in my life, if so what?)

Summary
============

In life on different planes we are put in a situation in which our further existence depends on the decision made. From the morning choice of clothes to decisions in which we sacrifice our own ideas, and even life. It is possible that even at the present moment we face a choice.

Difficult decisions are not impossible, there are many ways that facilitate them, but do I make the effort to face their realization?

   Once having made a choice, I must constantly choose

   -- Jerzy Liebert
