Making Decisions
################

Time of writing:
    Spring 2009
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of writing:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Open retreat for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    The diakonia discerned that retreats about discerning one's life path are needed. We decided to split the topic into two stages - "What am I looking for?" and "Making decisions". These retreats focused on the courage to decide and the ability to trust. We tried to fight a certain kind of paralysis that grips many people, that they are afraid to decide so that it won't be wrong. A frequently repeated sentence was "God blesses decisions". We led participants to the sentence: chosen means valuable. The retreat also undertook a fight with the misunderstood phrase "God has a wonderful plan for You" as if every decision of ours was planned, and we can only fit into the scheme from God or make a fatal error (here a simple comparison appeared: God is more like a GPS than a driver :).
