Faith Requires Questions
########################

Creation time:
    Spring 2007
Place of first retreat:
    Babice, Gliwice diocese
Place of creation:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Open retreat for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    Lent retreat aimed at showing faith as a journey - with moments when we see everything from the top and know where we are, but also with moments when we are in a valley through which we have to pass. We appreciated the role of doubts, which, well used, are a priceless gift for a believer. The retreat was inspired by Vladimir Holan's poem *Is* in which he writes: *"what is not shaky in it, is unstable"*.
