Responsibility for the Community
################################

Time of writing:
    Autumn 2005
Place of the first retreat:
    Miliardowice, Archdiocese of Katowice
Place of writing:
    Archdiocese of Katowice
Target group:
    Parish oasis, from pre-oasis to formation before 3rd degree
Retreat description:
    Parish retreat organized by the local diakonia aimed at integration and increasing awareness of responsibility for one's community. The retreat was created as part of traditional community integration trips. An idea arose to deepen integration by adding a formation thread. These were therefore (practically) the first weekend retreats we organized - hence only one group meeting. The feedback we received then from our participants became a motivation for us to develop this form.
