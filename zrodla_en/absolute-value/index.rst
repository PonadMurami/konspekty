Absolute Value
##############

Time of writing:
    Autumn 2013
Place of the first retreat:
    Babice, Diocese of Gliwice
Place of writing:
    Diocese of Gliwice
Target group:
    Open retreat for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    The retreat helps discover the dignity and potential of the human person before God and teaches how to defend one's values where one must stand firm as at Westerplatte. The final meeting calls for the responsible gift of time and sharing the values received with others, in the spirit of Christ's mission.
