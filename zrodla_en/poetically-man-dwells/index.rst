"Poetically man dwells" - Your everyday life - Your fascinations
##################################################################

Time of creation of outlines:
    Autumn 2009
Place of first retreats:
    Babice, Gliwice diocese
Place of creation of outlines:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Open retreats for people aged 13 to 23
Retreat description:
    Retreats about passion and everyday life. Based entirely on the analogy of a city - in which there are everyday spaces, related to the so-called "gray life" but also those that awaken wonder in us. We led participants to the thought that these two spaces do not fight with each other but coexist and are mutually dependent. The liturgical analogy is the procession with gifts - where what is everyday becomes extraordinary. However, this is a double analogy, because the gifts themselves are combined everyday life and celebration - water and wine. The key is peace between these two along with offering both to God. He sanctifies what is everyday and what is fascinating in the same way.
