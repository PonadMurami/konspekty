The Same Night
##############

Creation time:
    Autumn 2022
Place of first retreat:
    Hucisko, Częstochowa archdiocese
Place of creation:
    Gliwice diocese
Target group:
    Open retreat for adults
Retreat description:
    Every day we encounter different types of stories that have an impact on us. The stories passed on carry wisdom, tips, help us identify with the heroes, provide us with impressions and emotions. They shape who we are. Our retreat was a place where we stopped at the story that - as we believe - is the most important for us. A story of which we ourselves are participants. The story of The Same Night.
